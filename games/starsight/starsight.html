Eyes on the stars, pilot.

Two quick vision games (~2.5 min) watch for SANS, the eye changes many astronauts get in space. Each eye is compared with its own normal and with the other eye.

Screening and education demo, not a medical device and not validated. Screen tests can't diagnose SANS: they flag when to get a real eye scan. Data stays in this browser unless you copy it out.
